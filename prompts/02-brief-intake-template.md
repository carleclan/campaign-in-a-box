# 02 — Brief Intake Template

> **What this is:** the short form you fill in for each new campaign. Once the master
> system prompt is loaded, this is the *only* thing you send. It keeps inputs
> consistent, which keeps outputs consistent — the whole point of a "system".

> **Design note:** most people prompt an AI with a vague one-liner ("write me a gym
> promo") and get vague output. A structured brief forces the *human* to make the
> important decisions — who is this for, what do we want them to do — instead of
> leaving them to the model's guesswork. Good inputs are 80% of good outputs.

---

## The template (copy, fill, send)

```text
CAMPAIGN BRIEF
- Campaign name:
- Goal (what does success look like?):
- Target persona (Nervous Newcomer / Time-Poor Professional / Lapsed Member):
- The one action we want them to take:
- Offer or hook (if any):
- Key dates / deadline:
- Must-include details (price, times, venue, URL, terms):
- Deliverables (or "the full kit"):
- Anything to avoid this time:
```

## A filled-in example

```text
CAMPAIGN BRIEF
- Campaign name: January Fresh Start
- Goal: 30 free intro sessions booked in the first 3 weeks of January
- Target persona: Nervous Newcomer
- The one action we want them to take: book a free coached intro session
- Offer or hook: free intro + no joining fee if they start in January
- Key dates: runs 2–24 January; sessions bookable via website
- Must-include details: £39/month after; forgefitness.example/intro; no contract
- Deliverables: the full kit
- Anything to avoid this time: don't lean on "New Year New You" — we hate that
```

## Why the fields are what they are

- **One persona, one action** — the two fields that most improve output quality.
  Forcing a single target and a single call-to-action stops the copy hedging.
- **"Anything to avoid"** — a pressure valve. It lets you steer away from a cliché
  or a tired angle without rewriting the whole system.
- **"Must-include details"** — keeps the model factual. Everything here is treated
  as ground truth the model may not alter.
