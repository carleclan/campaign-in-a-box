# Brand Voice Guide — Forge Fitness

> This is the single source of truth the AI system reads before writing anything.
> It exists so that every piece of content — a tweet, an email, a flyer — sounds
> like the same brand, written by the same person.

---

## The business in one line

**Forge Fitness** is an independent strength & conditioning gym for ordinary people
who want to get stronger — not a chrome-and-mirrors "aesthetics" gym, and not a
budget box either. Coaching-led, community-first, no intimidation.

- **Location:** Riverside district, a mid-sized UK city
- **Price point:** mid-market (£39/month), positioned on *coaching quality*, not price
- **Size:** ~450 members, one location, 6 coaches
- **What makes it different:** every member gets a free coached intro; small-group
  strength classes; a genuinely welcoming, non-judgemental floor culture

## Who we are talking to (audience personas)

| Persona | Who they are | What's stopping them | What wins them over |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Nervous Newcomer" — Priya, 34** | Never been a "gym person", intimidated by equipment and fitter-looking members | Fear of judgement, not knowing what to do | Warmth, a clear first step, proof that beginners belong |
| **"Time-Poor Professional" — Marcus, 41** | Works long hours, wants efficient, effective sessions | No time to waste, needs it to fit his week | Efficiency, structure, results per hour |
| **"Lapsed Member" — Dawn, 47** | Was a member, drifted away, feels guilty | Guilt, lost momentum, assumes she's "started over" | Zero judgement, an easy on-ramp back, community pull |

## Voice: what we sound like

**Encouraging, straight-talking, and warm.** Like a good coach: honest with you,
never down on you. We believe fitness is for everybody and we say so.

### We ARE
- **Warm & inclusive** — "everyone starts somewhere" is our whole ethos
- **Plain-spoken** — short sentences, real words, no jargon
- **Motivating without hype** — we inspire action, we don't scream about it
- **Community-focused** — "we", "our members", "join us" over "you must"

### We are NOT
- ❌ **Bro-y or macho** — no "beast mode", "no pain no gain", "crush it", "grind"
- ❌ **Shaming** — never guilt people about their body, weight, or time off
- ❌ **Hypey / salesy** — no "🔥 LAST CHANCE 🔥", no fake scarcity, no ALL CAPS shouting
- ❌ **Clinical** — we're human, not a spreadsheet of BMI stats

## Style rules

- **Reading level:** aim for a 12-year-old could read it. Short words, short sentences.
- **Emoji:** sparing. One or two, tasteful (💪 🙌 ✅). Never a wall of them.
- **Exclamation marks:** at most one per piece. Enthusiasm comes from the words.
- **Person:** talk *with* the reader ("you"), talk *about* ourselves as "we".
- **Spelling:** UK English (e.g. "programme", "favourite", "centre").
- **Claims:** only promise what a real gym can deliver. No "lose 10kg in 2 weeks".

## Words we love / words we avoid

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| stronger, confident, welcome, coached, progress, community, at your pace | shredded, beast, grind, crush, shred, snap into shape, no excuses |
| everyone starts somewhere | new year new you |
| your first session | transformation challenge |
| a friendly place to train | elite / hardcore |

## The one-line promise (our north star)

> **"Get stronger, feel welcome — coaching for real people, at your pace."**

Every piece of content should ladder up to that promise.
